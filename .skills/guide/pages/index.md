# Roleplay: the overview

Roleplay is the story app of Molfar Vertep: you keep character cards, play chats with them, and let the app track the scene, remember the story and run the world around you. This page is a map. The app's own **Help** (in the rail) has a short article for nearly every screen; this guide explains how the parts fit together. Models and connections are not set up here but in the main Settings: see [[vertep:models]].

## Where things are

The rail on the left (a bar of tabs at the bottom on a phone) groups the sections:

- **Home** and **Chats**: continue where you stopped, or open any chat.
- **Characters**, **Groups**, **Marketplace**, **Personas**: the people. See [[characters]].
- **Lorebooks**, **Library**, **Canvas**, **World**, **Agents**: the story's memory and machinery. See [[memory]], [[litopys]], [[canvas]], [[world-dm]] and [[agents]].
- **Presets**: how the request to the model is built.
- **Shortcuts** and **Tools**: quick replies, regex, translation, pictures, voice, the Data Bank and more. See [[settings]].
- **Inbox** and **Help** at the bottom. The Inbox lists what waits on you: failed background jobs, Library suggestions and proposed souls.

Roleplay's own settings have no rail item. Open them with the "Roleplay settings" button in the Home header (on a phone, in the menu).

## Inside a chat

The chat header holds the story tools: **Note** (author's note), **Fired** (what went into the prompt, see [[memory]]), **Record** and **World**. The pill next to it opens tabs for Preset, Persona, Model and Agents of this chat. The "More" button on every message opens its actions, and the "+" menu next to the message box holds Polish, Regenerate with a hint, image generation and more.

Three helpers watch the story and cost extra model calls, so it is worth knowing them:

- The **Dashboard** reads each scene: [[dashboard]], with one input, the [[soul]] of a character.
- **Litopys** writes chapters and facts: [[litopys]].
- The **World DM** keeps a book of people and places: [[world-dm]].
