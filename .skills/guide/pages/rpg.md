# The RPG app: fights from a chat

Roleplay keeps the story; the optional **RPG** app plays a fight with rules and dice and sends back a short result. If you do not install RPG, nothing here appears to matter: the chat works as always. This page is for you if you want a fight with real numbers, injuries that last, and a result the story can use.

## Starting a fight

In the chat header press the **Fight** button (the sword). A window opens:

- **Your side**: you (the persona) and everyone the Dashboard shows in the scene, up to four. Nobody else is added, and nobody is picked from a card that is not in the scene.
- **Opponents**: press "Propose from the scene" and a model suggests one to four from the last reply and the party, or add them by hand with "Add an opponent". An opponent is a name, a few words, a level and a kind (all-rounder, brute, scout, archer, caster or tank). You never type numbers: the RPG app turns level and kind into strength, speed and health.
- **Stakes**: whether the party may flee, what a defeat costs (robbed, wounded, captured, deadly) and the setting (fantasy, cyberpunk and so on), which changes the words of the fight, not the numbers.

"Open the RPG app" writes the fight into Roleplay's own data and opens the RPG app on it. If the RPG app cannot be opened from here, the fight is still saved: open RPG yourself and it offers the fight.

## Coming back

When the fight ends, the RPG app saves the result and opens Roleplay on the same chat. The Fight button then shows a dot and the window **Fight result** shows who won, each fighter's health and injuries, the loot and a short summary. "Put the summary in the composer" places it in your message box as a stage direction (see [[first-story]] for the box); nothing is sent until you send it, and the story model writes the prose from those facts. "Mark as dealt with" clears the dot.

## Good to know

- The RPG app reads Roleplay's fight only after you allow it: open Home, the details of the RPG app, "Access to other apps". Roleplay reads RPG's results the same way.
- Neither app writes into the other's data. Injuries are kept by the RPG app and in the result; the Dashboard does not change by itself.
- An older engine opens the RPG app without telling it which fight: the RPG app then finds the waiting fight itself and offers it.
